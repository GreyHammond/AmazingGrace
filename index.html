Who We Are

Neighbors Helping Neighbors

Amazing Grace Outreach is a community outreach effort working alongside Lighthouse Baptist Church of Jackson and people throughout our community who simply want to help others.

It started with something simple: a few bottles of cold water handed to people who were struggling. Since then, it has grown into something much bigger.

We don't see race, religion, politics, or the mistakes someone has made. We see people. People who deserve dignity, compassion, and hope. Our goal isn't to judge where someone has been. Our goal is to help them take the next step forward.

Dignity

Every person deserves to be seen and treated with respect.

Compassion

We meet people where they are, without judgment.

Community

Together, we can accomplish far more than any one of us alone.

Hope

Every act of kindness can be the beginning of a new chapter.

"No one can help everyone, but everyone can help someone."

Our Mission

We Are the Bridge

Amazing Grace Outreach exists to reach individuals experiencing homelessness, poverty, isolation, and other forms of social disconnection through direct, relationship-based community outreach.

We are the boots on the ground. We meet people where they are, build relationships based on trust and dignity, identify barriers preventing them from accessing help, and advocate alongside them as they navigate the systems and organizations designed to serve them.

✗

We are not a shelter. But we help people find one.

✗

We are not a food pantry. But we connect people with food resources.

✗

We do not replace existing services. We complement them.

✓

We are the bridge. We connect disconnected people to the resources, services, and community they need.

Many people experiencing homelessness are not simply lacking a physical place to sleep. They may also be disconnected from family, friends, community institutions, employment, healthcare, government services, and other support systems.

For some, repeated negative experiences with institutions have created distrust. For others, the barriers are practical: no identification, no transportation, no phone, no documentation, no understanding of where to go, or no one willing to help them navigate a complicated system.

Amazing Grace Outreach works at that point of disconnection. We believe that existing resources can only help when people are able and willing to access them. Our job is to help close that gap.

How We Help

Our Outreach Model

Reach→ Listen→ Build Trust→ Identify Barriers→ Advocate→ Connect→ Follow Up→ Rebuild Community

Being the advocates means more than giving someone a list of phone numbers. It means building enough trust for someone to allow us to walk alongside them. It means listening before acting. It means understanding the individual's circumstances rather than assuming that one solution works for everyone.

That may mean helping someone obtain identification or other essential documents. It may mean helping someone find available food, shelter, healthcare, transportation, benefits, employment resources, or treatment. It may mean making a phone call, helping complete paperwork, accompanying someone through a difficult process, advocating when a barrier arises, or simply continuing to show up when someone has stopped believing that anyone will.

Sometimes the first successful intervention is simply showing up again.

Our objective is not simply to make a referral. Our objective is to help create a successful connection. We recognize that trust cannot be demanded and that meaningful relationships cannot be created through a single interaction.

Lasting stability requires more than access to emergency resources. People need relationships. They need people who know them, people they can call, places where they belong, and opportunities to contribute to the community around them. We don't simply want to help people survive the crisis. We want to help people become connected enough to move beyond it.
